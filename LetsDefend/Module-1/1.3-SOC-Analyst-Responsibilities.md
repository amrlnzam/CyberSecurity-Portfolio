# 🛡️ 1.3 SOC Analyst and Their Responsibilities

In this lesson, I learned about the core duties of a SOC Analyst and the essential skills required to succeed in this career.

### **What is a SOC Analyst?**
A SOC Analyst is the **first line of defense** and the first person to investigate threats to a system. Their primary role is to:
* Detect and investigate incoming threats.
* Triage alerts and determine if an incident is a real threat or a false alert.
* Escalate serious incidents to supervisors or higher tiers for mitigation when necessary.

---

### **Core Skills for Success**
To be an effective analyst, I need to master these three areas:
1. **Operating Systems:** Understanding what is "normal" in Windows or Linux to identify "abnormal" behavior.
2. **Network Fundamentals:** Ability to analyze IPs and URLs and detect potential data leaks.
3. **Malware Analysis:** Skills to determine the true purpose of malicious software and identify C2 servers.
